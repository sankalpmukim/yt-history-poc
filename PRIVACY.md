# Privacy Policy

YT Ledger is a browser extension that turns your YouTube watch history into a local ledger with categories and budgets.

**Everything stays in your browser.**

- YT Ledger has no server, no account system, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your ledger (watched videos, categories, rules, budgets and settings) is stored only in the browser's extension storage on your device.
- To record watch history, the extension reads the YouTube history page in your own logged-in browser session. To estimate watch time, it looks up public video durations on public YouTube watch pages. These requests go to YouTube only, like a normal page visit, and contain no data about you beyond the request itself.
- Nothing you watch, categorize or export is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.
- You can export your data (CSV/JSON) or delete it at any time from the dashboard; uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.

YT Ledger is open source (MIT); you can inspect exactly what it does at https://github.com/sankalpmukim/yt-history-poc.
